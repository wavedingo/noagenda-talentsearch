Getting started

Resources

Creating your first audio recording can feel intimidating, but we've got your back.

For your demo, all you need is an .mp3 audio file. Most computers have a built-in microphone, so you may already have everything you need to get started. Here are some free resources to help you record and create your file.

Every link below opens in a new tab.

Record your voice

  • How to record your voice with a microphone

    A beginner-friendly guide from Audacity covering the basics of recording your voice.

  • Audacity

    Free, open-source audio recording and editing software for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Going further

If you'd like to go beyond a basic recording and chase studio-quality sound, these go deeper.

  • School of Podcasting

    Free podcasting education — recording, editing, equipment and more.

  • Podfather Gear

    Adam Curry's original podcast setup, with instructions and links to the equipment used.

  • Curry Caster

    If you're more technical and feeling adventurous, Adam Curry's custom recording software.

None of this is required. A quiet room and the microphone you already own is enough — a tight five minutes recorded on a laptop beats a polished hour.
